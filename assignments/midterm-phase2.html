Student E-Portfolio Website Planning Document Name: Christopher Munford Course: CIS 207 - WCCS, Fall 2026 Assignment: Midterm Project, Phase 2 - Planning Your Student E-Portfolio Website Date: October 23, 2026 Deliverable 1: Website Purpose I want everything I do (school, projects, work experience) in one place online instead of scattered across folders and flash drives. I want a clean, simple website that makes it easy to find out what I do. I want visitors to walk away thinking I'm reliable, I keep learning, and I can apply what I learn in real-world situations. I also want to chart my progress from class to class and give employers a quick, straightforward way to review my background. I will emphasize my HTML/CSS work, early programming practice, and what I've learned about hardware and troubleshooting, along with real-world skills like communication, teamwork, and being on time. Deliverable 2: Target Audience My visitors will be employers and recruiters hiring for entry-level tech support and help desk positions, plus instructors and college staff monitoring my progress. Most visitors want the quick version: education, skills, examples, and contact info. When they leave, I want them to think I'm organized, serious about the field, and worth training.
