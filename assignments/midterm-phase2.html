Deliverable 1: Website Purpose

I want everything I do (school, projects, work experience) in one place online instead of scattered across folders and flash drives. I want a clean, simple website that makes it easy to find out what I do.

I want visitors to walk away thinking I'm reliable, I keep learning, and I can apply what I learn in real-world situations. I also want to chart my progress from class to class and give employers a quick, straightforward way to review my background.

I will emphasize my HTML/CSS work, early programming practice, and what I've learned about hardware and troubleshooting, along with real-world skills like communication, teamwork, and being on time.

Deliverable 2: Target Audience

My visitors will be employers and recruiters hiring for entry-level tech support and help desk positions, plus instructors and college staff monitoring my progress.

Most visitors want the quick version: education, skills, examples, and contact info. When they leave, I want them to think I'm organized, serious about the field, and worth training.

Deliverable 3: Website Content Outline

1. Home Page - index.html

  • My name, front and center
  • A short tagline: "IT student gaining skills for a career in the tech field."
  • A brief paragraph on what I'm studying and where I hope to go
  • A call to explore the site
  • A professional photo or a techy abstract image
  • A navigation bar linking to all pages

2. About Me Page - about.html

  • The background story behind my resume
  • My WCCS program and the most relevant classes, including CIS
  • Academic goals: finish my degree and improve at programming and web development
  • Career goals: get an entry-level job, then grow from there
  • Interests: hardware, how things work, troubleshooting, learning new skills
  • A few paragraphs of biography in my own words

3. Skills Page - skills.html

  • Technical skills: HTML/CSS, MS Office, computer hardware, beginner Git/GitHub, introductory C++
  • Professional skills: communication, teamwork, punctuality, problem solving, adaptability
  • Each skill marked Beginner, Developing, or Proficient, with no exaggeration

4. Projects Page - projects.html

  • Student E-Portfolio Website: six pages in HTML and CSS (layout, structure, navigation, styling, responsive design), with a screenshot and live link
  • C++ Console Program: loops, variables, logic, debugging, with a screenshot of the output and a link to the code
  • Hardware / Troubleshooting Guide: a problem I diagnosed and solved (technical writing, problem solving, hardware knowledge), with a photo or diagram and a link to the file
  • Each listing includes a name, brief description, tools, skills demonstrated, a photo or screenshot, and a link if relevant

5. Resume Page - resume.html

  • A web version of my resume
  • Education, with school and expected graduation date
  • Work experience, emphasizing reliability, safety, and teamwork
  • Technical and professional skills (condensed)
  • Certifications, awards, and accomplishments, if applicable
  • A link to the PDF version

6. Contact Page - contact.html

  • A professional email address, a GitHub link, and a LinkedIn link if I use it
  • A contact form, to be added in a later phase
  • No home address, phone number, passwords, or ID numbers

Deliverable 4: Visual Sitemap

The Home page (index.html) is the entry page. From the top menu, visitors can reach About, Skills, Projects, Resume, and Contact, and every page links to every other page through the same menu.

  • HOME - index.html
    • ABOUT ME - about.html
    • SKILLS - skills.html
    • PROJECTS - projects.html
    • RESUME - resume.html
    • CONTACT - contact.html
